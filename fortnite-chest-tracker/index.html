Where Fortnite shows your real chest total

Before you rely on any third-party tracker, know that the game already counts for you. It just does not put the number anywhere obvious.

  1. Load up Fortnite and choose Battle Royale.
  2. Open the Quests tab.
  3. Scroll to the Treasure Chest Hunt section and read the number listed there.

During a match you can also watch the counter in the top left corner of the screen, which ticks up as you open chests. That is the only figure Epic actually verifies — use it as your source of truth, and use the counter above for the per-match and per-day splits the game does not show you.

Chest types and what each one changes

Chest typeLookWhat is different
Standard chestWooden, gold trimBaseline loot. Three items, standard weapon pool.
Rare chestBlue glowHigher-tier weapons and heals from the same slot.
Sprite chestSeasonal skinAdds a Sprite on top of normal loot.
Buried chestUnder the groundNeeds a Treasure Map or a dig; often the bigger reward.

One thing worth stating plainly, because it is the most common misunderstanding in this season: the type changes the loot pool, not the token rate. Epic's published odds apply to every chest opened in a normal match — 1 in 137,500 for Pirate Shop Access, and 1 in 91,667 during Treasure Hours. Opening rare chests all night does not move those numbers.

One thing does help: the Fortune Chester Sprite has a chance to spawn extra loot from chests and to reveal nearby chests. It is the only in-game item that changes how many chests you find per minute.

How many chests per match is realistic

A lobby holds 80 players on the same 110+ spawn points, so reaching every marker is not a plan. The realistic band:

PaceChests per matchWhat it looks like
Casual drop3–8One POI, fight whoever lands with you.
Deliberate route30Two full POIs without detouring.
Clean lobby, tight route50First two POIs uncontested and no third-party fight.

Multiply that out and the tracker starts to mean something. At 30 chests a match, you are looking at roughly 83 matches to reach the 50% mark for a Pirate Shop Token — which is exactly the kind of arithmetic the counter above is for. Full probability table lives on the token chance calculator.

Questions people actually ask

What is a Fortnite chest tracker?

A counter you tap each time you open a chest, so you can see how many chests you have opened in a session, a day, or the whole season. It does not connect to the game — every count is entered manually by you.

Can a chest tracker read my in-game chest count automatically?

No. Epic does not expose chest counts through an API, so no website can read them. Any tracker that shows your real in-game total is asking you to type it in. For the official number, open Battle Royale, go to Quests, and scroll to the Treasure Chest Hunt section.

How many chests are on the Fortnite map?

More than 110 Treasure Chest spawn points in Chapter 7 Mini-Season 1, according to a GameRant map survey published November 1, 2026. Each marker covers a cluster of chests rather than a single chest.

Which location has the most chests?

Lantern Landing, with more than 46 Treasure Chest spawns. Arlong Park is the strongest second stop with 39 spawns and sits directly west of Lantern Landing.

How many chests can you open in one match?

A lobby holds 80 players, so you cannot reach all 110 spawn points in one match. With a tight route, 30 chests per match is routine and 50 is possible.

Where can I see how many chests I have opened?

Open Battle Royale, select the Quests tab, and scroll to the Treasure Chest Hunt section, which lists your opened chests. The counter also appears in the top left corner during a match.

Do all chest types drop the Pirate Token?

Yes. Epic's published odds apply to every chest opened in a normal match: 1 in 137,500 for Pirate Shop Access, and 1 in 91,667 during Treasure Hours. The chest type changes the loot pool, not the token rate.

Does this work in Zero Build?

Chests spawn in both Battle Royale and Zero Build, and the spawn points are the same. Your route matters more than the mode.
