Pirate Token Chance Calculator

Pirate Shop Access drops from 1 in 137,500 treasure chests. This calculator turns that rate into the number that actually matters: your chance of getting at least one token from the chests you will really open before the season ends on December 5, 2026.

Quick answer

  • 1 in 137,500 chests per roll — 0.00073% each.
  • ~95,300 chests for a 50% chance, ~316,600 for 90%.
  • During a Treasure Hour at 1.5x the rate becomes 1 in 91,667, so 50% falls to ~63,500 chests.
  • No pity timer. An empty chest never improves the next roll.

Pirate token odds: what the formula is doing

The chance of at least one drop from n chests is the complement of missing every time:

P = 1 − (1 − 1/137,500)n

It is a curve, not a line. The first 10,000 chests move you about 7%. The next 85,000 move you another 43%. That is why "just keep opening" feels like nothing is happening for hours: it is, mathematically, nothing happening.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Pirate token odds per chest?

1 in 137,500, or 0.00073%. During a Treasure Hour at 1.5x it is 1 in 91,667 (0.0011%).

Is the drop rate per chest or per match?

Per chest. A match with 20 chests gives you 20 separate rolls at 1 in 137,500 — the calculator multiplies out to that total.

Can I buy Pirate Shop Access?

No. There is no shop listing, no bundle and no legitimate third-party sale. Anything offering to sell you the token or "guarantee" the drop is a scam.

Is there a free item shop token for it?

No. The only route in is the chest drop. Epic has not given the token away through the item shop, a code, or a creator promotion.

Does my chest counter reset?

There is no counter to reset — the game does not track failed attempts. Each chest is rolled independently, so a bad streak carries no debt and no credit.

How many chests do I need before the season ends?

The season ends December 5, 2026. At 15 chests per match and 5 matches a day you get about 75 chests a day — roughly 1,650 over the remaining three weeks, which is a 1.2% chance. That is the honest number, and it is exactly why the Treasure Hour window is worth planning around.
