How the calculation works

Two lines of arithmetic, nothing hidden:

XP still needed = (target level − current level) × 80,000 − XP already banked toward the next level

XP per day = XP still needed ÷ days left in the season

Example as of November 13, 2026: if you are level 40 with 0 XP banked and want level 100 in 22 days: 60 levels × 80,000 = 4,800,000 XP, divided by 22 days = 218,000 XP a day. The calculator does the same thing for whatever numbers you type.

Where your XP actually comes from

Knowing the total is half the job; the other half is knowing which activity pays it fastest. Ranked by XP per minute, roughly:

Quests win on pace, Creative wins on convenience, and normal matches fill whatever is left. The trap most players fall into is grinding a single source: Creative alone caps out well before level 100, and matches alone can take longer than the remaining season.

One more thing worth checking before you plan a chest grind: chests pay noticeably more inside Treasure Hour, which runs in two-hour blocks on Saturdays. If you are close to your daily target, moving your play session into one of those windows is the cheapest XP you will find all week. Frequently asked questions

How much XP is one level in Fortnite?

80,000 XP. It does not scale — level 2 costs the same as level 99. That flat rate is what makes planning possible at all: multiply the levels you want by 80,000 and you have your answer.

Is 80,000 XP per level an official Epic number?

No, and it is worth being direct about that. Epic does not publish a per-level XP table. The figure comes from community testing and is consistent across the independent trackers that rebuild their tables every season. It is reliable enough to plan around, but it is not a document Epic handed out.

How many levels is 1,000,000 XP?

12.5 levels. 1,000,000 ÷ 80,000 = 12.5. Useful shorthand: 800,000 XP is 10 levels, 4,000,000 XP is 50 levels.

Do XP maps count toward battle pass levels?

Yes. Creative XP feeds the same season level that unlocks battle pass rewards, so an XP map is a legitimate route. The catch is the weekly cap — once you hit it, the map keeps running but the bar stops moving. Community reports put the Chapter 7 cap at 60 levels a week, though Epic adjusts it between seasons.

Does the XP requirement change after level 100?

No. Every level past 100 still costs 80,000 XP. Level 200 is 15,920,000 XP from a standing start, which is why very few players finish a season above 150.

How much XP do I need per day to reach level 100?

Example as of November 13, 2026: from level 1 with 22 days left, 360,000 XP a day. From a realistic mid-season position like level 50, it is 178,000 a day.
