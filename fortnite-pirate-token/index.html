Fortnite Pirate Token: What It Is, How to Get It & Drop Rate

Updated November 24, 2026 · Treasure Hunters season reference

What is the Pirate Token in Fortnite?

The Fortnite Pirate Token is the community name for Pirate Shop Access, a rare Treasure Hunters chest reward. Activating access lets you claim eligible rotating Item Shop offers for a 14-day period. It is not a code you can buy or a currency you earn through a fixed number of matches.

1 in 137,500 per chest

That is approximately 0.000727% in a normal match, according to Epic's published odds. The listed Treasure Hour boost is 1.5×; verify the current event before playing.

How to get the Fortnite Pirate Token

  1. Enter the Treasure Hunters Battle Royale event and open eligible chests while looting.
  2. Watch for the Pirate Shop Access reward notification. Opening more chests adds attempts; no route guarantees a drop.
  3. If you receive access, follow the Pirate Shop prompt in the Item Shop. Review your account's eligible activation dates before confirming.
  4. After activation, return during your 14-day window to claim eligible offers currently in rotation.

Use the chest locations and manual tracker to record openings. Check the Treasure Hour schedule for announced boosted sessions.

What Pirate Shop Access includes

Published player guides describe two access windows, one for 2026 and another for 2027. Read the in-game dates for your reward: the season finale, the activation deadline and your personal 14-day period are different dates. Access covers eligible V-Bucks Item Shop offers in rotation; it does not unlock every cosmetic ever released. Real-money packs, gifting and Battle Pass levels are excluded; additional item restrictions can apply.

Access and activation reference: VGC's Pirate Shop Access guide. Your in-game reward screen controls eligibility.

Pirate Token drop rate: single chest versus a whole session

The next chest still has the same base chance after a long losing streak. Under an independent fixed-rate model, 1,000 normal openings give about 0.725% chance of at least one drop. At 137,500 openings the cumulative chance is about 63.2%, not 100%.

The searchable token odds table and population estimator explains the maths. For your own match pace, use the existing token chance calculator.

Season availability

Epic's Treasure Hunters announcement names December 5, 2026 as the Chapter 7 end-event date. This guide describes that event; it does not promise the token will remain obtainable in later seasons.

Frequently asked questions

Can I guarantee a Pirate Token after 137,500 chests?

No. 1 in 137,500 is a per-chest probability, not a pity counter. Under a fixed independent model, that many openings gives approximately a 63.2% chance of at least one drop.

Does a One Piece skin improve Pirate Token odds?

Epic’s published reward table does not list a skin-based modifier. Use the announced event multiplier rather than assuming an outfit changes your odds.

How many players have received the Pirate Token?

We do not have a verified worldwide winner count. Our token odds page estimates hypothetical recipients from user-entered player and chest counts; it is not a live total.
