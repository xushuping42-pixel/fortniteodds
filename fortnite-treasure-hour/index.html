Fortnite Treasure Hour Times: Nov 14, 2026

Treasure Hour runs 2:00–4:00 PM ET and 9:00–11:00 PM ET on Saturday, November 14, 2026. Pirate Shop Access odds are boosted 1.5x in the first hour and 2x in the second. Here are both windows in your time zone.

Quick answer

  • Window 1: 2:00–4:00 PM ET — 19:00–21:00 UTC
  • Window 2: 9:00–11:00 PM ET — 02:00–04:00 UTC (Nov 15)
  • Pirate Shop Access: 1 in 137,500 → 1 in 91,667 (1.5x) → 1 in 68,750 (2x)
  • Also boosted: Weekly Gold Outfit 7.5x, vehicles and decals 10x

How many chests do you need to open?

At the normal 1 in 137,500 rate, you need about 95,300 chests for a 50% chance of Pirate Shop Access, and about 316,600 chests for 90%. During Treasure Hour the first-hour rate of 1 in 91,667 cuts that to roughly 63,500 boxes for 50% and 211,100 for 90%; the second-hour 1 in 68,750 rate brings it to about 47,650 and 158,300.

That is why players stack chests instead of grinding raw. Work out your own numbers with the Pirate Token chance calculator, then log your progress with the chest tracker. The full drop table is on the drop rates page.

Frequently asked questions

What time is Fortnite Treasure Hour today?

Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 2:00–4:00 PM ET and again at 9:00–11:00 PM ET. In UTC that is 19:00–21:00 and 02:00–04:00 the next day.

How big is the Pirate Shop Access boost?

1.5x during the first hour, so 1 in 91,667 instead of 1 in 137,500. The second hour goes to 2x, or 1 in 68,750.

Do I have to opt in to Treasure Hour?

No. It activates on its own at the scheduled time. Just play and open chests during the window; the boosted rates apply automatically.

How many Treasure Hours are left in Chapter 7?

Three: November 14, November 21 and November 28. The season ends on December 5, 2026.

Does Treasure Hour change XP or chest spawn rates?

No. It only changes drop odds inside chests. Chest spawn locations and the 80,000 XP per level rate stay the same.
