Frequently asked questions

Is a “power hour” the same as a Treasure Hour?

Yes. “Power hour” is what players called it before Epic used the official name Treasure Hour. Same two-hour window, same multipliers.

How long is each Treasure Hour session?

Two hours. There are two sessions per Saturday, one in the US afternoon and one in the US evening, which is why European and Asian players get one session late at night and one the next morning.

Does the multiplier apply to the Pirate Shop Token?

Yes — Pirate Shop Access is boosted 1.5x during Treasure Hour, from 1 in 137,500 to 1 in 91,667. It is not boosted outside those two hours.

Do I have to be in a specific mode?

The multiplier applies to normal chest spawns across the standard battle royale modes. It does not carry into Creative maps or custom games.

When is the next Treasure Hour after November 14?

Saturday, November 21, 2026, at the same two times (2–4 PM ET and 9–11 PM ET) unless Epic changes the slot.
