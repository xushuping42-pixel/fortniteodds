Method and limits

For players who have not yet received access, let p = multiplier / 137500, n = chests per player, and N = players. Individual chance is q = 1 − (1 − p)^n. Expected recipients are N × q. Expected drop rolls N × n × p are a different quantity and must not be labelled unique winners.

The model counts players with at least one successful personal roll. It does not model squad-shared grants, changing multipliers, different opening counts between players, or reward removal after a first win. A world total cannot be inferred without those inputs. A long losing streak does not make the next chest more likely to succeed.

Frequently asked questions

Are these official Fortnite winner statistics?

No. Epic is the source for the per-chest rates. Every cumulative chance and recipient figure on this page is a mathematical estimate from explicit assumptions, not a count of actual winners.

Is 1 in 91,667 exact?

It is rounded. The model uses 1.5 divided by 137,500, not a rounded denominator. Future event multipliers may differ.
